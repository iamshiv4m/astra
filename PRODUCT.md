# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Clients seeking personal astrology guidance, professional astrologers managing consultation schedules, and stakeholders evaluating a product pitch.

## Product Purpose

Make one-to-one consultation discovery, booking and conversation feel calm, trustworthy and personal. Success is a complete clickable journey, with a connected astrologer workspace and consistent locally persisted state.

## Positioning

A premium wellness/teleconsultation experience, not a traditional astrology portal. ASTRA: "Clarity, one conversation away."

## Operating Context

Mobile-first responsive web in English, with Indian currency and dates in Asia/Kolkata. Demo presenters can start a future session immediately using a clearly labeled shortcut without changing its booked time.

## Capabilities and Constraints

All public, client, booking, astrologer, consultation and technical architecture routes from the user brief are required. Auth, payment, video and chat delivery are simulated. There is no production backend, independent advisor verification or real customer endorsement.

## Brand Commitments

Original ASTRA text logo, contemporary typography, restrained celestial motifs and generous spacing. The latest user direction is modern and a little more Indian-centric: indigo, saffron, crisp whites and purposeful linear gradients, rather than dull beige or an old-fashioned editorial theme. Indian context appears through an original illustrative kundli, Vedic/traditional approach discovery, familiar life questions and real language filters. The homepage tells a connected story and invites participation. No copied competitor branding, excessive religious imagery or guaranteed predictions.

## Evidence on Hand

The user's complete source brief and approved implementation plan define requirements. Profiles, testimonials, reviews and trust claims are illustrative content, not independent evidence. Stock portraits are not images of real ASTRA consultants.

## Product Principles

- Make the complete conversation journey work without dead ends.
- Keep availability, booking, consultation and dashboard data coherent.
- Distinguish simulation from operational production capability.
- Let clarity, accessibility and restrained craft carry trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, labeled forms, reduced motion, appropriate contrast, accessible dialogs and mobile chat sheets. Target responsive widths are 375, 390, 430, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels.
